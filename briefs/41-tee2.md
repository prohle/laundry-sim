# Brief 41 — unblock the tee

Implement task **7.7** with its two unblock notes.

## 1. The joint range

The upper joint limit is `-2.4`. The tee hem needs `-2.400` to `-2.402`. That limit was an arbitrary
choice when the arm was built, not a physical constraint. Widen it to **`[-2.6, 1.0]`** in `sim/arms.py`
and say so in the code comment.

Do not widen any other joint.

## 2. The fold line

The brief's `y = 0.05` was wrong: the hems are at the back (y ≈ −0.30) and the shoulders at the front
(y ≈ 0.40). A half fold carries the hem **to the shoulder line**, so the carry target is `y = 0.40`.

The tee is wider than the towel because of the sleeves, so use `width >= 0.85` for the tee (already in
the code as `TEE_WIDTH_MIN`), `depth <= 0.60`, moved half at least 3 mm above the stationary half.
